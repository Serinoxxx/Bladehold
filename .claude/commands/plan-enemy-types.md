description: Plan components/CSVs for the enemy-type roster and (if feasible) auto-wire prefabs
---

Plan the components and csvs required for these enemy types. If possible also build an automated enemy wiring script to add all the necessary components to the enemy and build the prefabs.

Dwarf: Swarm unit. Extremely high move speed, low HP.

Ancient Warrior: Standard balanced unit.

Ancient Queen: High armor (takes less damage from light attacks).

Troll: Meat shield. High HP, slow move speed.

Big Ork: Standard heavy unit. High damage, medium speed.

Spirit Demon: Floating ghost. Ignores collision with other enemies to prevent body-blocking.

Forest Guardian: Shoots fast, straight-line projectiles.

Mystic: Shoots slow, homing energy orbs.

Evil God: Bullet Hell. Fires projectiles in a 360-degree radial burst.

Pig Butcher: The Hook. Fires a fast projectile that pulls the player toward him.

Medusa: Directional Aura. Casts a cone that halves player move speed.

Forest Witch: Support Aura. Heals or buffs the defense of nearby enemies.

Mutant Guy: Death Trigger. Drops a toxic AoE damage pool when killed.

Slayer: Telegraphs a red line, then dashes instantly, damaging anything in the path.

Mechanical Golem: Revs up and charges, bouncing off arena walls like a pinball.

Dark Elf: Agile assassin. Uses a dash component to randomly dodge laterally when targeted.

Red Demon: Leap & Slam. Jumps to the player's position and triggers a massive circular AoE.

Barbarian Giant: Whirlwind. Becomes a moving AoE hitbox that destroys player projectiles.

Elemental Golem: Fissure. Slams the ground, triggering three delayed AoE cones that track outward.

Fort Golem: Spawner. Moves incredibly slowly, but spawns 3 Dwarves every 5 seconds.
