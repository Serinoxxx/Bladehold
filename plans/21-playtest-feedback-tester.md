## Phase 5: Ultimate redesign (Arcane Cores and the ultimate wheel)

**Design (decided with Lance 2026-10-07):**
- **No charge meter.** Ultimates are activated by spending an **Arcane Core**.
- **No buying ultimates.** Each held weapon's ultimate is always unlocked, so you have the melee ultimate of your melee weapon and the ranged ultimate of your ranged weapon. Each activation costs 1 Arcane Core.
- **Arcane Cores** are an in-run currency: reset in `StartNewRun`, carried between sectors in `RunSession`. They come from:
  - **captains**, which drop cores;
