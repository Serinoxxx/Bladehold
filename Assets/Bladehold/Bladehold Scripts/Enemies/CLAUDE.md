# Enemies

## Roster

- **`Assets/Bladehold/Config/Enemies.csv`** is parsed by `EnemyRosterSO`. Columns:
  `id,displayName,health,damage,minGold,maxGold,speed,scale,unlockWave,spawnChance,minSpawn,maxConcurrent,knockbackResistance,enabled,minThreat`
  - Blank stat cells keep the prefab's SO value.
  - `enabled` = 0 removes the row.
  - `minThreat` = first campaign tier the type joins sector waves at; 0/blank = never in sector waves. `unlockWave` is the wave within the 5-wave sector.
  - The first row (goblin) is the roster's fallback type; sector fodder is `fodderEnemyId` on the pacing asset.
- **`EnemyPrefabMapSO`** (`EnemyPrefabMap.asset`) maps CSV ids to prefabs. Variants are generated by `Editor/EnemyPrefabGenerator.cs` from `Editor/EnemyManifest.cs`; see the `generate-enemy-prefabs` skill. Don't hand-wire them.
- **Spawning in sectors**: `SurvivorsSpawner` admits a type when the sector's threat (node tier) is at least `minThreat` and the wave is at least `unlockWave`, with a 60% goblin floor. T1 is goblin/brute/bubbler/big ork/bomber; Bannerman and Powder Keg join at T2, Bulwark and Assassin at T3, Storm Witch at T4, Troll at T5. See `Waves/CLAUDE.md` → Sector difficulty.

## Behaviour

- Shared pieces:
  - `AIMovement` (NavMesh chase, staggered repaths, avoidance tiers).
  - `AIAnimation` + `LocomotionAnimator` (Synty blend params).
  - `AITargetSelector`.
  - `AIAttack` (basic melee).
  - `CoinDropper`, `Enemy` (kill reporting).
- **Attacks are uneven by design.** Special enemies have telegraphed attacks (Bulwark counter-slam, Kombusta dynamite, Troll slam, bomber, and others). Basic enemies just swing for minor damage when in range, with no telegraph.
- **`Enemy.RosterId`** is stamped by `EnemyDefinitionApplier` on every roster spawn (null for scene-placed enemies). Use it to pick out plain goblins (e.g. `WardenEscort` recruits only `goblin`).
- **Non-goblin bodies get their own simple controller**, not the goblin one: the goblin clips hunch other rigs over. `Bladehold Animations/Dwarf AC.controller` and `Medusa AC.controller` are a Locomotion blend (Idle/Walk/Run on `MoveSpeed`) plus Death, Knockdown→`GetUp`, Stagger, and Attack/Cheer layers, and they declare every goblin parameter so `LocomotionAnimator` never hits a missing one. Assign via the manifest's `animatorOverridePath`. The generator also adds `EnemyAnimationEvents` to the rig to swallow third-party clip events.
- **Baked crowd (fodder goblin + skeletons).** `Crowd/BakedCrowdAgent` (wired by the manifest's `CrowdComponents`) switches the rig's Animator, SkinnedMeshRenderer and held props off at spawn, and `Crowd/BakedCrowdRenderer` (created on demand) draws every baked enemy with `Graphics.RenderMeshInstanced`, one group per `Bladehold Animations/Crowd/<Name> Crowd Animation.asset`: idle, run, attack and attack-while-running poses in a bone-matrix texture, skinned on the GPU by `Bladehold Shaders/BakedCrowdLit.shader`, which wraps the stock URP Lit passes. AI, NavMesh, attacks and colliders are untouched.
  - **Crowd types** are the baker's `Targets` list: `goblin` plus four skeletons (`skeleton_soldier`, `skeleton_soldier_shield`, `skeleton_knight`, `skeleton_knight_shield`). The skeletons are manifest variants of `Goblin Enemy Variant` itself: the Synty goblin rig carries the skeleton bodies as switched-off alternates, so the manifest's `bodyName` just shows a different body and rig, ragdoll and crowd setup carry over. They swing a sword with `Skeleton Sword AC` (a copy of `Goblin Lean AC` whose Attack state plays Synty's `A_Attack_LightCombo01A_Sword`) and share the goblin's `AIAttackSO` (its 0.4 s wind-up matches the swing's hit). Golden/impulse are removed.
  - **Shielded skeletons** (`skeleton_soldier_shield`, `skeleton_knight_shield`) carry `ProjectileResistance` (`Enemies/Skeletons/SkeletonShieldProjectileResistanceSO`): every projectile hit (bow and tower arrows, thrown axes, wand bolts) does ×0.5, from any direction. Ballista bolts (`piercesShields`) and melee hit at full. They're not "shielders": the spawner's 2-at-once bubbler cap only matches `bubbler` now.
  - **Held props** (manifest `props`: a sword on `Hand_R`, a shield on `Hand_L`) are real MeshRenderers on the live rig, and the baker merges them into the crowd mesh, skinned fully to their bone. They must share the body's material (`PolygonDungeon_01`): the crowd draws one material per type. The agent's `propRenderers` hides them while baked.
  - New crowd type: a manifest entry with `components = CrowdComponents("<Name>")` (and a non-override `AnimatorController` with the goblin's state names), a `Targets` entry in `Editor/BakedCrowdBaker`, then Generate Enemy Prefabs → Bake Crowd Animations. `BakedCrowdBaker.EnsureDataAsset` creates the empty data asset the generator wires before the first bake.
  - A goblin **promotes** back to the real rig, posed from its baked frame, when it takes non-lethal damage, is knocked down, the player dies (cheer), it turns golden/impulse, or it gains an enabled `HighlightEffect`; every real ragdoll also promotes first (`EnemyRagdoll.BuildIfNeeded`). Anything new that needs the real Animator or material on a plain goblin must trigger a promotion (call `BakedCrowdAgent.Promote()`), or it will be invisible on baked goblins.
  - A promoted goblin **rejoins** the crowd (`Demote`) after `demoteAfterSeconds` without damage, back in plain locomotion, and either off-screen or beyond `demoteMinCameraDistance` (the pose snap stays unseen).
  - **Past the ragdoll cap** (`EnemyRagdoll.MaxActive`, the Max Ragdolls setting) both deaths and non-lethal flings play a **baked fall** (`PlayBakedFall`) instead of a knockdown / animated death. Up to the cap they're real ragdolls.
    - The falls are real ragdoll simulations the baker recorded **root-relative** (pelvis travel stripped). A pooled `Crowd/BakedFallBody` (one rotation-locked sphere Rigidbody on the Ragdoll layer: hits the world, not goblins or the player) takes the real launch velocity and carries the root; the clip does the tumble. While the sphere is airborne longer than the recording, playback holds half-way down. Blood bursts/decals play at the recorded landings, raycast onto the real ground.
    - A death stays down as a baked corpse. A fling (`KnockbackReceiver.BakedFlingRoutine`) picks a fall that **lands face-up** (the only get-up clip starts face-up), samples the NavMesh at the stand-up spot (none → dies, like a real fling), plays that fall's **aligned get-up** (the GetUp clip turned/shifted at bake time to start where the fall landed), then `FinishGetUp` moves the root to where the body stands and it rejoins the crowd's idle without a pose jump.
    - `BakedCrowdAgent` doesn't promote on a hit that `KnockbackReceiver.WouldFling`, so the fall starts from the baked pose; `Promote` is a no-op mid-fall. Golden/impulse goblins never fall baked.
    - Bake: the first 6 variations are the deaths; more are recorded until 4 land face-up. Tunables (sphere radius/friction, settle, timeout, get-up crossfade) are under "Baked falls" on the crowd SO.
  - `AIAttack.OnAttackStarted` drives the baked attack; `SlowStatus`'s `animator.speed` still scales baked playback.
  - Re-bake with **Bladehold/Crowd/Bake Crowd Animations** (`Editor/BakedCrowdBaker`, every type in about 10 s) after changing a crowd enemy's body, props, rig, ragdoll or those clips. The SO's playback tuning survives a re-bake.
- Special attack components sit beside their SOs (`*Attack.cs` + `*AttackSO.cs`), with per-enemy folders for bigger kits (`Bulwark/`, `Bannerman/`, `Bomber/`, `Assassin/`, `Captain/`, …).

## Captains

- `Captain/CaptainKombustaController` (dynamite barrage at range, fire aura up close), `Captain/CaptainEnemyController` (Fraglob: war cry, stomp, bulwark stance; no prefab authored yet) and `Captain/CaptainMograController` (Mogra Hexfang, goblin shaman: rune patterns from `HexRunePatterns`, hex bolts, Hex Step blink, bone totems whose rings you dash through, a breakable Bonefire Ritual; three HP phases).
- All implement `ICaptain`; `CaptainRegistry` (both in `Captain/ICaptain.cs`) maps display names to roster ids. `GameLoopManager.SpawnCaptainForWave` spawns them on wave 5 and on 3-skull cards. There's no Fraglob prefab yet: an empty `captainPrefab` logs an error and spawns Kombusta instead.
- Mogra's model is the Sidekick character `Bladehold Sidekick Characters/Mogra`. His spell prefabs, VFX, MMF feedbacks, cast bar, staff, rig ragdoll (copied from the goblin base) and `Mogra AC` are built by `Editor/CaptainMograBuilder` (Bladehold/Captains/Build Captain Mogra Assets); re-run it rather than hand-editing them. Every piece of one cast shares a `HexHitGroup`, so overlapping runes or bolts hit once.
